# Sunrest mining remnants

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for the cave progression update. The intact transparent source is `source.png`. Native exports simplify the artwork to match Retri's 16-pixel world scale: 16×16 miner views, a 22×16 cart, 18×12 tools, 12×16 lantern/crate, and a 24×16 rubble pile. Exports use nearest-neighbor scaling, solid pixel alpha, and a common foot baseline within each asset. `preview.png` shows the in-game native art enlarged for inspection.

The miner is a friendly NPC near Shalehorn's door; props are inspectable mining remnants on the main progression route. Rubble replaces the visible entrance after the opening collapse. No dialogue is baked into images.

## Exact generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Production sprite sheet for a minimalist 16-pixel-tile retro RPG crystal mining cave. Exactly SIX isolated pixel-art assets in a THREE-COLUMN by TWO-ROW grid, equal cells, generous transparent gutters, no labels or borders. Top row left: a weary friendly MINER NPC, front/down facing, human adult, dusty brown clothes, muted ochre mining helmet with a tiny lamp, short beard, small pick on belt. Top row middle: SAME miner NPC facing left. Top row right: a small abandoned wooden mining cart with two wheels and a little dull blue ore inside. Bottom row left: a discarded mining pick and a short broken timber, lying together. Bottom row middle: one small warm amber lantern on a low crate. Bottom row right: a low pile of angular cave rubble suitable for blocking a narrow tunnel. No characters except the two requested miner views. Art direction: simple chunky crisp SNES-like pixel art designed for about 16x16 native-pixel NPCs and 16-24 pixel props, matching tiny top-down RPG overworld sprites. Friendly understated earthy palette, restrained detail, no realistic shading, no huge glows. True transparent background. No floor, shadows outside the object, grass, scenery, UI, lettering, or gridlines. Objects must be complete, centered in their own cells and consistently drawn at low detail.
