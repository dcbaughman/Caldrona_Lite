# The Depths battle art

Emberwraith is a lost adventurer's spirit. It swallowed a spider egg and drew its fire inward, where it can burn without setting the surrounding silk alight. The Silk Sarcophagus is another lost adventurer, whose spirit animates the cocoon around them.

The built-in ImageGen tool created the sources from the user's sketches and the game's existing battle art. The shipped sprites are fitted to 46 × 36 and 22 × 46 native-pixel cells with transparent edges. The backdrop uses a 480 × 160 pixel grid doubled to fit the battle field. Broodfire uses a warm palette on the existing Ember Arc frames.
