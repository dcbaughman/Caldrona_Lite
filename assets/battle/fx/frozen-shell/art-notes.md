# Frozen crystal overlay

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool, using the user's Frozen Adventurer Crystal Animation.gif as the visual reference. The reference was inspected across its six frames: ground shards rise and close into a complete crystal around the adventurer.

The generated source contains ice only, not a replacement character. Native transparent exports are 44×48 pixels. Blue facets are partially transparent so each existing character or monster remains visible. A common crop and foot baseline preserve the growth animation.

Frames 0–3 form the crystal (80 ms each). Frames 3–5 shimmer while the actual Frozen status remains active. Frames 6–7 break apart on thaw or cure (140 ms each). The runtime aligns the shell to the target's feet and camera offset, scales larger monsters, and clears it on KO or battle exit. Impact-timed effects defer the initial pop until contact.

`source.png` is preserved intact. `prepare_frozen_fx.py` packs native frames, applies the translucent compositing treatment, and produces `sheet.png`, `manifest.json`, and the character-composited preview GIF. The runtime uses only Tk, with cached images and persistent canvas items.

## Exact generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent pixel-art battle VFX sprite sheet, ICE ONLY, to overlay existing characters in a retro RPG. Reference: the attached frozen-adventurer animation, especially its final faceted crystal capsule enclosing the whole body with frost spikes around the feet. DO NOT draw any adventurer, creature, person, body, clothing, floor, background, frame, or text. Generate an exact FOUR-COLUMN by TWO-ROW sheet of EIGHT equally sized animation cells with a fixed common center and foot baseline. Row-major sequence: 0 small icy ground spikes and a few chips popping upward; 1 rising shards reaching knee height; 2 facets closing around the torso with open top; 3 full tall faceted crystal capsule enclosing an empty character-shaped space; 4 same full capsule with subtle glint; 5 same full capsule with another subtle glint; 6 capsule cracked, beginning to split; 7 several small scattered ice fragments breaking outward, mostly empty. All geometry aligned and constant scale across frames. The finished full crystal should be a relatively compact upright hexagonal ice shell with clear angular pale-cyan and white rim, blue inner facets, tiny jagged base spikes. The center should be transparent or VERY translucent so the game's existing character remains visible underneath. This must be a true transparent-background overlay, not an illustration with a character frozen inside. Medium: crisp chunky pixel art, designed to read at about 40x48 native pixels per cell, restrained blue/cyan/white palette, hard pixel edges, no soft glows. Avoid complex gigantic particle effects. Wide transparent gutters between all eight cells, no dividers or labels.
