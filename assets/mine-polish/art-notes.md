# Sunrest cave polish

The two source sheets were made with built-in ImageGen using the contact sheet in `style-reference.png`, made from Caldrona Lite’s original overworld sprites and cave tiles. The native PNGs use nearest-neighbor scaling. Temur and Merren keep their existing front sprites. The entrance states share a crop and registration point in the wall.

## NPC sheet prompt

Generate a transparent asset atlas for original retro JRPG Caldrona Lite, using the attached actual game sprite comparison as the style reference. Precise 4-column by 3-row grid, one separate figure per equal cell and generous transparent gutters; NO written labels, grid dividers, backgrounds, drop shadows, lighting or glow. Each row is one consistent person and views in exactly this order: facing viewer (down), facing left, facing right, facing away (up). Row one: NEW adult cave miner with narrow shoulders, dark steel-blue jacket, leather straps and subdued brass lamp on charcoal helmet. Work-worn, compact, serious, demure expression, thin silhouette and same proportions as dark-haired Temur in attached reference. Row two: TEMUR faithfully based on reference's dark swept hair, small slim silhouette, dark navy torso, single blue-gray shoulder stripe, pale face and boots. Row three: MERREN faithfully based on reference's auburn wide-brimmed hat and rust-brown merchant coat, compact thin silhouette. Preserve their costume identities in every direction. Every sprite designed for a 16 by 16 canvas with only about 9 opaque pixels wide and 13 opaque pixels tall. Draw enlarged blocks of native pixel art, sparse clusters, near-black outline that blends into dark indigo, condensed late 16-bit JRPG overworld proportions. Tightly controlled color ramp and sharp silhouette, no antialias, no gradients, no mascot look, no oversized heads or round facial features. All full-body idle sprites, exactly shared scale and aligned feet. Transparent RGBA background.

## Sealed passage (reusable wall prop)

`prepare_sealed_passage.py` builds the iron forecourt gate with a slate frame and the sealed stair. Both rise into the two-tile wall from a single centered approach tile. `glyph-stair.png` is a strip of 32 × 32 frames with a straight run of dressed stone steps and a softly pulsing purple glyph. The quarry and the Depths share the one-tile south exit threshold from `den_art.py`, which carries the cave floor into the opening. The rubble variants remain available as `sealed-passage.png` and `sealed-passage-marked.png`.

The supplied Jacole, Karnak, Carwen and Tule maps are references for door proportions, ascending stairs and exits from the lower edge of a room.

## Selvar inscription (reusable wall prop)

`prepare_selvar_inscription.py` builds the slate panel on a 32 × 32 native canvas. The gray overworld glyph and the beveled panel are both centered over the inspection tile. The cut state adds pale Veskar lines, stone dust and Temur's charcoal.

## Old Workings sidequest

`prepare_lower_workings.py` draws everything for Larvel's sidequest (`lower_workings.py`) in the cave's own stone, timber and slate ramps:

- `workings/stair-up.png` (32 × 32): rock-cut steps climbing back up to the side drift, framed in timber, with Larvel's signal rope hanging down the right side.
- `workings/ledger-post.png` and `ledger-post-bare.png` (16 × 32): a squared pit prop in warm timber with wavy grain, a knot and a split. It stands on a sill block under a cap beam with iron spikes. - `workings/stele-r1.png` … `stele-r6.png`, each with a `-rubbed` twin (24 × 40, centred on its tile): a slate marker with rounded shoulders, a dark side face, a carved border and a fieldstone plinth. The words come from `RUBBING_FJALORA` in `lower_workings.py`. After a rubbing, charcoal hazes the face, the letters glow pale blue, and a charcoal stub is left on the plinth.
- `workings/selvar-door.png` (32 × 32): a slate slab between carved pillars, with three glyphs and a blue seam down the middle. It covers the two-row wall between the Seal Hall and the Crew Refuge until it opens.
- `workings/floor-crack.png` and `knocker-burrow.png` (32 × 32): the heaved floor in the Knocking Gallery, and the hole left after the Old Knocker is beaten.
- `assets/hesk-overworld.png`: Larvel's walk sheet recoloured for Hesk, the crew's tally-keeper, with a slate cap and a rust coat.
- `assets/battle/old-knocker.png` (26 × 30, about 260 × 300 in battle at 10× zoom): the mini-boss, pixelated from the concept art in `old-knocker-source.png`. It is a hunched, tube-mouthed borer on four clawed legs. It is kept very chunky on purpose: only 30 native pixels tall, with broad, soft shading. The script floods away the source backdrop, averages the creature down, lifts the dark render, and cuts it to 12 colours. Ink stays only on the shadow side, below and to the right of the body, so the lit top-left edges stay open against the cave. It also adds a soft top-left rim and a dark mouth ring with blue-stone light in the throat. The battle stands it near the bottom of the field (`KNOCKER_GROUND`) so it fits.
- `assets/items/accessories/pit_lantern.png` (32 × 32): Larvel's lamp, the rescue reward.
