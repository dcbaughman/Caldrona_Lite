# Sunrest cave polish

The two source sheets were made with built-in ImageGen using the contact sheet in `style-reference.png`, made from Caldrona Lite’s original overworld sprites and cave tiles. The native PNGs use nearest-neighbor scaling. Temur and Merren keep their existing front sprites. The entrance states share a crop and registration point in the wall.

## NPC sheet prompt

Each row is one consistent person and views in exactly this order: facing viewer (down), facing left, facing right, facing away (up). Row one: NEW adult cave miner with narrow shoulders, dark steel-blue jacket, leather straps and subdued brass lamp on charcoal helmet. Work-worn, compact, serious, demure expression, thin silhouette and same proportions as dark-haired Temur in attached reference. Row two: TEMUR faithfully based on reference's dark swept hair, small slim silhouette, dark navy torso, single blue-gray shoulder stripe, pale face and boots. Row three: MERREN faithfully based on reference's auburn wide-brimmed hat and rust-brown merchant coat, compact thin silhouette. Preserve their costume identities in every direction. Every sprite designed for a 16 by 16 canvas with only about 9 opaque pixels wide and 13 opaque pixels tall. Draw enlarged blocks of native pixel art, sparse clusters, near-black outline that blends into dark indigo, condensed late 16-bit JRPG overworld proportions. Tightly controlled color ramp and sharp silhouette, no antialias, no gradients, no mascot look, no oversized heads or round facial features. All full-body idle sprites, exactly shared scale and aligned feet. Transparent RGBA background.

## Sealed passage (reusable wall prop)

`sealed-passage.png` and `sealed-passage-marked.png` are hand-placed pixel art generated by `prepare_sealed_passage.py`. They share `quarry-door.png`'s 24 × 19 footprint and registration: centre the image on a north-wall tile (`(tile + .5) * TILE`) and it sits in the wall row exactly like the door. The arch stone is sampled from the door's surround; fallen slabs use a cooler blue-grey ramp so the blockage reads apart from the frame. The marked state adds pale fresh crosscuts and is used for the fallen stair in the Sunrest forecourt (`MAINTENANCE_ALCOVE` in `cave_story.py`).

## Selvar inscription (reusable wall prop)

`selvar-inscription.png` and `selvar-inscription-cut.png` are generated by `prepare_selvar_inscription.py`: a 12 × 22 patch of worn purple slate with a natural crack and one column of Selvar script (hook, stem, zig, diamond, tail), drawn after `selvar-inscription-source.svg`. Rows 0–17 are the slate; anchor its top two pixels above the wall row, like the quarry door's crown. Rows 18–21 hold the cut state's pale dust and Temur's charcoal on the floor line. The cut state adds two fresh Veskar cuts; where they cross a groove they pick up the blue crystal light. In the Sunrest forecourt it sits between Shalehorn's door and the sealed stair, which is now flush with the room's north-east corner (`SELVAR_SEAL_AT` / `SEALED_STAIR_AT` in `cave_story.py`).

## Selvar close-up

`selvar-closeup.png` (196 × 110 native, shown at SCALE above the dialogue box) is generated by `prepare_selvar_closeup.py` from `selvar-inscription-source.svg`. Each stacked column of the SVG is flattened to strokes, carved as hard-edged pixel grooves (shadowed upper wall, lit lower lip), and set left to right in source order on a worn slate tablet with a natural crack. Two fresh Veskar cuts cross the whole face and glint blue where they cut old grooves. Edit the SVG and re-run the script to change the text.

## Larvel and Rando

`prepare_larvel_twins.py` builds three files from Larvel's original art, which it leaves untouched (`assets/portraits/larvel.png`, `assets/larvel-overworld.png`):

- `assets/portraits/larvel-v2.png`: the game's Larvel portrait. Same image with the sharp lower corner of the nose tip rounded off by a small bevel.
- `assets/portraits/rando.png`: Larvel's twin. Larvel v2 with his tousled hair swapped for a bard's bowl cut (smooth dome, centre part from crown to hairline, fringe combed away from the part, side panel curled under past the ear) and a bushy mustache, both in Larvel's own hair ramp, then flipped so he faces the other way.
- `assets/rando-overworld.png`: Larvel's walk sheet with the pale chin pixels on the front and side views turned into a two-pixel mustache. The lantern glow is left alone.
